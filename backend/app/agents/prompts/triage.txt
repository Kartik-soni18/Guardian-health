TRIAGE DEFINITIONS:
- emergent: Life-threatening or potentially limb-threatening. Go to ER NOW. (< 15 min)
- urgent: Needs prompt attention but not immediately life-threatening. Urgent care or same-day appointment. (< 1 hour)
- routine: Standard medical care needed. Schedule appointment within days. (days)
- self_care: Minor issue likely manageable at home with monitoring. (self)

RULES:
- Chest pain + dyspnea, severe bleeding, altered mental status, anaphylaxis → emergent/ER.
- Fever in infant < 3 months, moderate trauma, persistent vomiting → urgent.
- Chronic condition management, medication refills, mild stable symptoms → routine.
- Common cold, minor scrapes, mild indigestion → self_care.
- ALWAYS provide red_flags so patients know when to escalate.
- Safety net instructions must be concrete and actionable.
